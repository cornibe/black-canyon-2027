FRI • FEB 12

Travel and race preparation

5:00–6:00 a.m. Leave home for PHL. 7:00–8:00 a.m. nonstop departure target. 10:00–11:00 a.m. MST arrive PHX, collect rental car. Noon–2:00 p.m. lunch and required bib pickup. 3:00 p.m. check into Anthem. 4:00–6:00 p.m. groceries, drop bags and early dinner. 8:30 p.m. lights out.

SAT • FEB 13

Race day and family outing

4:45–5:30 a.m. runner breakfast. 5:45–6:45 a.m. official shuttle to Mayer, depending on wave. 7:00–8:00 a.m. race start. Family has breakfast and chooses Lake Pleasant, Cave Creek Regional Park, Spur Cross/Cave Creek or Wickenburg. Runner finishes and takes the official shuttle back to Anthem.

SUN • FEB 14

Recovery and flexible family day

Sleep late and keep activity gentle. After checkout, choose Lake Pleasant, Black Canyon Heritage Park or Cave Creek. For the red-eye plan, leave Anthem around 8:00 p.m. For the Monday plan, stay a third night.

MON • FEB 15

Two return choices

Sunday-night plan: arrive PHL around 6:00–7:00 a.m. and home around 7:00–8:00 a.m. Monday plan: take a morning or midday nonstop and arrive PHL in the late afternoon or evening.

Target the first morning nonstop, ideally leaving PHL near 7:00 a.m. and reaching PHX around 10:00–11:00 a.m. local time. This protects the Friday bib-pickup deadline.

Option A: Sunday-night red-eye

Depart PHX around 10:00–11:30 p.m. Sunday and arrive PHL around 5:30–7:00 a.m. Monday. Minimizes time away, but is less comfortable after a 100K.

Option B: Monday daytime nonstop

Stay Sunday night and fly Monday morning or midday. Expect a late-afternoon or evening arrival in Philadelphia. Adds one hotel night but provides much better recovery.

Current route schedules show roughly 5–7 daily nonstops each way. Verify exact February 12, 14 and 15, 2027 flights before booking.
