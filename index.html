Trip schedule

The shortest practical family itinerary that still protects race logistics and provides a genuine Arizona experience.

FRI • FEB 12

Fly, collect bib, settle into Anthem

Take an early nonstop PHL–PHX, collect the rental car, stop at the official expo and required bib pickup, then check into Anthem. Buy breakfast and race supplies before an early dinner.

SAT • FEB 13

Race day + independent family adventure

Runner takes the official Anthem shuttle to the start. Family chooses a full or half-day nature outing without needing to follow the race, then returns to Anthem.

SUN • FEB 14

Sleep late, enjoy a flexible local outing and depart PHX on the Sunday-night nonstop. Consider retaining the room through Sunday evening if the price is reasonable.

Family nature options

Selected for a six-year-old, distinctive Arizona scenery, reasonable driving from Anthem and no requirement to spectate.

Best overall

Spur Cross Ranch + Cave Creek

Start with an easy out-and-back on the Jewel of the Creek area, where desert foothills, saguaros, shade and a seasonal creek create more variety than an exposed desert walk. Keep the hike flexible—about 60 to 90 minutes total—then explore Cave Creek’s compact Western-style center for lunch, shops and relaxed wandering.

About 40 minutesHalf to full dayFlexible hiking
Official siteGoogle Maps
Best small-town day

Wickenburg + Hassayampa Preserve

The preserve is a surprising ribbon of cottonwoods, water and short shaded trails in the desert. Pair it with historic downtown Wickenburg, outdoor sculptures and lunch. Add Vulture City only when the family wants a longer day centered on mining history and restored frontier buildings.

About 70–80 minutesFull dayNature + history

Lowest-stress option

Lake Pleasant Regional Park

The closest substantial nature destination offers desert-and-water scenery, short shoreline walks, picnic areas and a discovery center. It is ideal when the family wants an unhurried morning, outdoor time and the freedom to return to the hotel whenever energy drops.

About 25 minutesHalf dayEasy to shorten
Official siteGoogle Maps
Best easy desert park

Cave Creek Regional Park

A more structured alternative to Spur Cross, with a nature center, family-friendly short trails, broad Sonoran Desert views and a playground. It is a good choice when the child wants variety and the adults want a straightforward park with clear facilities and minimal planning.

About 30 minutesHalf dayNature center + trails
Official siteGoogle Maps
Closest local outing

Black Canyon Heritage Park

This small community park provides an accessible nature trail, bird and butterfly habitat, interpretive signs and local history. Combine it with the Old Cañon School Museum when open and pie or lunch at Rock Springs Café. It is not a major attraction, but it is distinctive, close and easy after a slow morning.

About 20–25 minutesTwo to four hoursGentle walking

Optional history extension

Vulture City Ghost Town

A restored mining settlement south of Wickenburg with original and reconstructed buildings, artifacts and open desert surroundings. It works best as an add-on to Wickenburg for a child interested in old machinery, mines and frontier stories. Confirm hours before committing to the drive.

Hotel and flights

Anthem is the efficiency choice. It minimizes race-morning complexity and keeps the family near several local outings.

Recommended hotel

Hampton Inn Phoenix / Anthem

Across from the Outlets at Anthem race transportation area, with free parking, breakfast and an outdoor pool. Book Friday and Saturday nights; ask about late checkout or retaining the room Sunday for recovery before the red-eye.

Flight strategy

Nonstop PHL–PHX both ways

Outbound: choose Friday’s earliest practical nonstop, ideally reaching Phoenix before noon to protect the bib-pickup deadline. Return: take the Sunday-night nonstop, typically arriving Philadelphia around 6:00–7:00 a.m. Monday. Thursday evening is the safer outbound option if winter weather risk is concerning.
